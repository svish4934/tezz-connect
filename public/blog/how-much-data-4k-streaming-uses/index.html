How Much Data Does 4K Streaming Actually Use?

Estimate how much data 4K video can consume, why usage differs by streaming service and how to manage playback quality.

4K streaming can use substantially more data than standard-definition video, but there is no single data-per-hour figure for every service. The amount depends on the platform, video bitrate, content, playback settings and whether the stream adapts to the available connection.

A useful published example

Netflix lists up to 7 GB per hour for Ultra HD (4K) playback under its High data-usage setting. This is a service-specific upper estimate, not a universal rule for every 4K stream or platform.

Turn an hourly estimate into a monthly plan

Using the Netflix figure as an illustration, two hours at 7 GB per hour could use up to about 14 GB. If that happened every day for 30 days, the simple upper estimate would be about 420 GB. Actual use may be lower or different because playback quality and bitrates change.

Why 4K data use changes

A high-motion scene can need a different bitrate from a static scene. Streaming services also use compression, adaptive bitrate delivery and different encoding profiles. Some services may offer more than one quality setting, and the same title can be delivered at different bitrates on different devices.

Check the service and device settings

Look for playback-quality or data-usage controls in the streaming account, app or device. Some services allow per-profile settings; others adapt quality automatically. A TV set to 4K output does not mean every video is being streamed in native 4K.

Ways to manage data use

Choose a lower playback setting when using a capped mobile connection, download content over a trusted unlimited or high-capacity connection when the service permits it, and check whether automatic previews or background playback are enabled. If several people stream at once, include all devices when estimating household usage.

What this means for broadband

A 4K stream needs enough sustained throughput for smooth playback, but the internet speed number alone does not determine data consumption. If you have a monthly data cap, estimate viewing hours across all users and leave room for updates, backups, gaming downloads and other connected devices.
