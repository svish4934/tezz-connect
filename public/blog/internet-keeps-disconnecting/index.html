Internet Problems & Fixes

Why Does My Internet Keep Disconnecting?

Connection drops can originate from Wi-Fi, your router, modem or ONT, cabling, device settings or the ISP network. Use the symptoms to narrow the cause.

Identify what is disconnecting

Check whether only one device loses access or every device disconnects. If Wi-Fi disappears but a wired device remains online, the wireless network may be involved. If all devices lose internet while still connected to Wi-Fi, the router may have lost its connection to the modem, ONT or ISP. These clues help separate a device problem from a wider outage.

Check router, modem and ONT indicators

Observe the equipment lights when the connection drops and note which indicators change. A power light going out may suggest a power or adapter issue. A broadband, DSL, cable or optical indicator changing state can point toward the access connection. Light meanings differ by model, so consult the device manual rather than assuming a colour means the same thing on every router.

Inspect cables, power and placement

Check that power adapters and network cables are firmly connected and undamaged. Avoid repeatedly bending fibre cables. Keep equipment ventilated and away from heat or moisture. If the router restarts by itself, note whether it happens during heavy use or at regular intervals; overheating, a failing adapter or hardware faults may be involved.

Rule out Wi-Fi and device causes

If only one device disconnects, update its operating system and wireless drivers, check power-saving settings and test another device in the same location. If all wireless devices drop but Ethernet works, investigate Wi-Fi interference, router placement and wireless settings. Avoid changing several advanced settings at once.

Record outages and recurring patterns

Record the date and time of every drop, how long it lasts, which devices are affected and what the router lights show. Check the ISP’s outage notices or app. If drops happen at similar times, note household activity and whether equipment is restarting. A written log is more useful to support teams than a general report that the internet is unreliable.

Troubleshoot in a safe order

Restart equipment only according to the manufacturer or ISP instructions, and allow it to reconnect fully. Do not factory-reset the router unless you have the configuration details and know how to restore service. If the ISP supplied the equipment, contact them before changing WAN, optical or authentication settings.

When to contact your provider

Contact the ISP if every device loses internet, the broadband or optical indicator repeatedly shows a fault, or wired connections also drop. Share timestamps, indicator behaviour, equipment model and error messages. Ask for a line or access-network check and keep the complaint reference.

Key takeaway

Repeated disconnections can have several causes. Identify whether the failure affects one device, Wi-Fi only or the entire connection, then use timestamps and equipment indicators to guide the next step.
