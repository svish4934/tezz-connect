OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

How TEZZ CONNECT approaches research, sourcing, accuracy and transparency across its internet education articles.

Research and sources

Articles should use relevant primary or authoritative sources where available, such as regulator guidance, standards documentation, manufacturer support pages and service-provider information. Sources are linked within the article when they support a specific explanation or recommendation. Claims must be represented in context, and regulatory guidance must be identified by jurisdiction.

Originality and independent presentation

Articles and substantial rewrites are expected to be drafted independently in TEZZ CONNECT's own explanatory voice. Research is used to verify facts, not to copy another publisher's wording, outline, examples, tables or distinctive presentation. External claims, data, specifications and recommendations should be attributed near the relevant explanation. The publication standard prohibits invented statistics, test results, credentials, testimonials and citations. A phrase search can help identify close matches, but it is not a comprehensive plagiarism check and cannot establish that an article has zero similarity to other material.

Accuracy and technical context

Technical explanations should distinguish theoretical capabilities from typical real-world performance. Network speed, latency, coverage and reliability can vary with the device, connection type, local network conditions, provider, routing and test method. Examples must not be presented as universal results or provider guarantees.

Review and updates

Content should be checked for technical accuracy, working references and relevant metadata before publication. Updates should be made when a meaningful correction, product change, technical development or change in source guidance affects the article. We do not display a publication or last-reviewed date where a reliable editorial record is unavailable.

Authorship and expertise

The TEZZ CONNECT Blog is published by TEZZ CONNECT. Unless an article explicitly identifies an individual contributor, publisher attribution should not be interpreted as a claim of individual professional certification or independent expert review.

Corrections and transparency

Readers can report a possible error, outdated claim, broken source or unclear explanation by emailing tezzconnect.contact@gmail.com. Please include the article URL, the relevant passage and, where possible, a reliable supporting source. Reports are assessed against the cited source and current information; an article is corrected when a change is warranted. Material changes should be reflected in the article when a reliable update record is available. We aim to distinguish sourced facts, general educational guidance and product-specific information.

Use of research and editorial tools

Research and writing tools may support topic exploration, organisation and editing, but they do not replace source verification, original explanation or human editorial responsibility. No tool output should be treated as a source of truth without checking the underlying evidence. We do not claim that automated checks prove originality or guarantee accuracy.

Commercial independence

References to third-party products or services do not by themselves imply endorsement. Any future affiliate or sponsored relationship should be clearly disclosed where it is relevant to the content.

For background about the publication, see About the TEZZ CONNECT Blog. For limits on the information provided, read the Blog Disclaimer.
