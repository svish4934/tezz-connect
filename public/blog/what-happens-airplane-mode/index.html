Internet in Everyday Life

What Happens When You Turn On Airplane Mode?

Understand what airplane mode switches off, why Wi-Fi or Bluetooth may still work, and what to check before flying.

Airplane mode changes how a phone uses its wireless radios. It is designed to disable cellular transmission, but it does not turn a smartphone into a completely offline device. The exact behaviour depends on the phone, its software and the airline’s rules.

What airplane mode normally changes

When enabled, airplane mode turns off the phone’s cellular connection so it does not register on a mobile network for ordinary calls, texts or mobile data. On many devices, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are also switched off initially, but they can often be turned back on separately.

Can you use Wi-Fi during a flight?

Some airlines provide in-flight Wi-Fi and allow passengers to use it while airplane mode remains enabled. Availability, permitted services and the point at which you may connect depend on the airline and crew instructions. Do not assume that a device setting overrides aviation rules.

What about Bluetooth headphones and accessories?

Bluetooth may be available after you enable airplane mode, allowing supported headphones or accessories to reconnect. Check the airline’s instructions and your device’s current status rather than relying on how it behaved on another flight.

Does airplane mode save battery or stop roaming charges?

It can reduce battery use by preventing the phone from searching for cellular service, and it can help prevent accidental mobile-network use while travelling. It is not a substitute for checking roaming settings: if you manually re-enable cellular service or use another SIM, charges may still apply.

Why does behaviour differ between phones?

Apple documents that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth can be turned back on while airplane mode is active. Android manufacturers and software versions may present different controls. After changing settings, check the status icons and confirm that cellular service remains off if that is what you need.

A safe pre-flight check

Follow the airline’s instructions, enable airplane mode when directed, and only turn Wi-Fi or Bluetooth back on if permitted. If your goal is to avoid roaming charges, also review mobile-data and data-roaming settings before departure.
