Why Is Wi-Fi Slower on Your Phone Than on Your Laptop?

Phones and laptops can perform differently on the same Wi-Fi network because their wireless hardware and software are not identical.

Different wireless hardware

A laptop and a phone may support different Wi-Fi generations, frequency bands, channel widths, antenna designs and spatial streams. Even devices using the same Wi-Fi standard can have different radio capabilities, so their real-world throughput may differ.

Band, distance and interference

The 2.4 GHz band often reaches farther but may be crowded. 5 GHz and 6 GHz can offer more capacity where supported, usually with shorter practical range. Your phone may connect to a different band or access point than your laptop. Walls, distance and nearby networks also affect results.

Power saving and background activity

Phones may use power-saving behaviour that affects wireless performance. Background app updates, cloud photo uploads, VPNs and data-saving settings can influence observed speed. Check whether the difference remains after pausing background activity and disabling any VPN for the test, if appropriate.

Compare under the same conditions

Place both devices in the same location, connect them to the same Wi-Fi network and band if possible, and use the same test service and server. Run several tests close together in time. Make sure neither device is downloading updates or backing up files.

Check settings before blaming the router

Install current operating-system updates, check data-saving and battery settings, and review whether a VPN or security app is active. If only the phone is slow in identical conditions, investigate its model-specific Wi-Fi capabilities and settings before replacing network equipment.

When the router may be responsible

If both devices are slow in the same area but improve near the router, coverage or interference may be involved. If the laptop is consistently fast and the phone remains slow under controlled conditions, the phone itself is a more likely place to investigate.

Bottom line

A phone can be slower than a laptop because the devices have different radios, software and usage conditions. A controlled comparison helps identify which difference matters.
