Make the test fair: Record what changed and compare under similar conditions. Pause large downloads, keep the same device and location where possible, and avoid changing several settings at once.

Three useful rules before you troubleshoot

1. Compare more than one device

If every device has the same problem, investigate the router, Wi-Fi environment or internet service. If only one device is affected, start with that device.

2. Separate Wi-Fi from the internet connection

Wi-Fi is the wireless link between your device and router. Your internet service is the connection from the router or modem to your provider. A problem in either place can make websites feel unavailable.

3. Change one variable at a time

Move closer to the router, test again, then try a wired connection if available. Changing many things together makes it difficult to identify what actually helped.
