YOUR STEP-BY-STEP LEARNING JOURNEY

Learn the internet, from the basics to confident troubleshooting.

A practical internet learning roadmap for everyday users. Start with how the internet works, understand speed and Wi-Fi, then build the skills to investigate connection problems and make better networking decisions.

Free learning path · Self-paced · Beginner to advanced

9 learning steps3 levelsNo sign-in required
HOW TO USE THIS ROADMAP

Learn one idea, then try it.

Follow the lessons in order if you are new. If you already know the basics, jump to the level that matches your goal. Open each guide, try its activity where available, and tick the step when you feel comfortable explaining the idea in your own words.

You do not need special equipment or technical experience. The interactive tools are learning activities; they do not measure your live connection unless a page clearly says it is running a real test.

01

Beginner: understand the connection

Build a simple mental model of how internet access and speed work.

Understand how your device, router, internet provider and a website server work together.

Read: What is the internet? →
FOUNDATION

Learn what download speed, upload speed and Mbps mean, and why file sizes use different units.

Read: Internet speed explained →
SPEED

See how sending files, cloud backups, live streaming and video calls use your upload connection.

Read: Upload speed explained →
UPLOAD

Try this: Explain the difference between downloading a video and uploading a photo. Then look at the download and upload figures from a speed test and identify which is higher.

02

Practical: diagnose and improve your home network

Use a repeatable process before changing settings or spending money.

Separate a whole-connection issue from a single-device issue, weak Wi-Fi coverage or an app-specific problem.

Open: Connection troubleshooter →
DIAGNOSE

Understand router placement, walls, interference and the difference between 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz where supported.

Read and try: Improve Wi-Fi speed →
WI-FI

Learn what routers, extenders, mesh systems, Ethernet access points and network switches actually do.

Explore: Networking buying guides →
EQUIPMENT

Try this: Pick one room where Wi-Fi is unreliable. Compare the connection near the router and in that room, then use the troubleshooting guide before deciding whether new equipment is necessary.

03

Advanced: understand responsiveness and network behaviour

Go beyond download speed and learn why connections can feel inconsistent.

Understand response time, variation between responses and packet loss, especially for calls and gaming.

Read and experiment: Latency and jitter →
PERFORMANCE

Learn why server distance, stable response time and packet loss can matter as much as bandwidth.

Read: Internet speed for gaming →
REAL-WORLD

Change example network conditions and observe how Wi-Fi bands, layouts and response behaviour can affect a connection.

Open: Interactive network lab →
PRACTICE

Try this: Change one setting at a time in the latency simulator or network lab. Describe what changed and why. These are simplified teaching models, not measurements of your home network.

KEEP BUILDING YOUR KNOWLEDGE

Finished the roadmap? Put it into practice.

Run a real speed test when you want to measure your current connection. Use the guides to understand the result, compare conditions carefully and decide what to investigate next.
