Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Site operator: Suraj Vishwakarma

This policy explains how TEZZ CONNECT handles information when you visit tezzconnect.online and use its internet tools and Knowledge Hub. TEZZ CONNECT is operated by Suraj Vishwakarma. For privacy questions, data requests or formal grievances, contact the Grievance Officer, Suraj Vishwakarma, at tezzconnect.contact@gmail.com. Please use the subject line “Privacy grievance”.

Information processed

  • Connection and request data: when a page or test endpoint is requested, our hosting and network providers (including Cloudflare) may process IP address, approximate network location, ASN, browser/request information, timestamps and security or diagnostic logs to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the service. The site does not ask for GPS coordinates or your street address.
  • Speed-test measurements: your browser sends test requests to TEZZ CONNECT endpoints. Download/upload throughput and HTTP response timing are measured to our network edge. These measurements can be associated with request and device/network metadata processed by the hosting provider.
  • Optional community benchmarks: benchmark sharing is off by default. If you switch it on, a completed test may send limited measurement summaries and coarse country/region information for aggregate regional comparisons. Published regions require at least 30 contributions in the recent seven-day window. You can switch sharing off at any time; this stops future contributions.
  • Information stored on your device: test history and language preference are stored in browser local storage. They are not synced between your devices and can be cleared through your browser settings. Benchmark preference is also stored locally.
  • Analytics and advertising: the site loads Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Google and its partners may process identifiers, device/browser data, usage events and cookie or similar technology information to provide measurement, prevent abuse and serve or measure ads. Their processing is also subject to Google's own terms and privacy disclosures.
  • Messages: if you email us, we process the details and contents you provide to respond and maintain relevant correspondence.

Purposes and sharing

We use the information above to operate the speed test and site, provide requested diagnostics, maintain security, understand site usage, display and measure advertising, respond to enquiries, and produce regional statistics where you have opted in. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product. Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, security, analytics and advertising, or disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

International processing and retention

Our hosting, analytics and advertising providers may process information in countries outside your own. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider settings. TEZZ CONNECT does not promise a fixed retention period for provider security logs or third-party analytics/advertising data; please consult the relevant provider policies for their retention practices. We retain correspondence only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can clear local browser storage, disable optional benchmark sharing, manage cookies through browser settings, and use available Google ad and analytics controls. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided directly, or to raise a privacy concern. Please include enough detail for us to locate the relevant information; do not email passwords or sensitive identity documents unless we specifically explain a secure method. We will review and respond within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.

Children

TEZZ CONNECT is a general-audience utility and information site, not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
