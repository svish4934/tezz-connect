# TEZZ CONNECT Content Editorial Standard

Status: Required for all new and updated Knowledge Hub guides and Blog articles.

## 1. Originality is mandatory

- Write every article from a blank draft in TEZZ CONNECT's own voice, with an original outline suited to the reader's question.
- Do not copy, scrape, spin, lightly rewrite, or closely paraphrase another website's article, wording, headings, examples, tables, diagrams, or distinctive structure.
- Do not imitate a competitor's article flow or distinctive framing. Common technical terms and unavoidable factual concepts may be used, but the explanation and presentation must be independently created.
- Do not reuse another publisher's images, charts, illustrations, or code without a valid licence and required attribution. Prefer original diagrams, examples, calculations, and tools.
- Translation must preserve meaning while being written naturally for the target language; do not machine-spin or copy a third-party translation.

## 2. Research without copying

- Research facts using primary and authoritative sources first: standards bodies, official product documentation, government/regulator publications, peer-reviewed research, and the relevant service provider's own support documentation.
- Record the source URL, the specific claim it supports, and the date checked for facts that can change.
- Close the source before drafting the explanation. Write the concept in your own words and use an original example relevant to the reader.
- Attribute external data, recommendations, quotations, benchmarks, and technical specifications with a nearby link. Clearly label vendor-specific requirements as vendor-specific.
- Never invent statistics, test results, benchmarks, experts, author credentials, testimonials, or citations. Separate measured TEZZ CONNECT data from illustrative examples and estimates.

## 3. Human-first, SEO, AEO and GEO

- Answer the reader's main question early in plain language.
- Explain technical terms, use concrete examples, and give practical steps that can be followed.
- Match the search intent naturally. Use descriptive titles, headings, internal links, metadata, and structured data only when accurate and useful.
- Make important facts, definitions, methodology, limitations, and source attribution clear enough for readers and answer engines to interpret correctly.
- Add original value where it genuinely helps: TEZZ CONNECT measurements and methodology, transparent calculations, original diagrams, useful comparisons, troubleshooting steps, or interactive tools.
- Do not keyword-stuff, pad articles, manufacture FAQs, or write for search engines at the expense of the reader. Never promise rankings or AI citations.

## 4. Required originality review before publication

For every new article and substantial rewrite:

1. Search the web for several distinctive phrases from the draft in quotation marks, including the opening, a middle section, and any unusual explanation or analogy.
2. Search for the article's main topic plus its planned heading/outline to identify unusually close competitor structures.
3. Compare any close matches manually. Rewrite independently if wording, sequence, examples, table structure, or explanation is substantially similar beyond ordinary technical language.
4. Check all quotations and factual claims against their original sources. Add attribution or remove unsupported claims.
5. Confirm that diagrams, screenshots, examples, and calculations are original or appropriately licensed.
6. Record the reviewer, review date, search phrases checked, sources used, and any changes in the article's editorial record or pull-request description.

A web phrase search is a screening step, not a complete plagiarism database. Do not claim that an article is plagiarism-free or guarantee zero similarity unless an appropriate comprehensive check has actually been performed. Common technical definitions and independently expressed facts may naturally resemble other sources.

## 5. Pull-request publication checklist

- [ ] Draft written independently; no copied or spun passages.
- [ ] Distinctive phrase and outline searches completed; close matches reviewed.
- [ ] External claims verified and sources attributed.
- [ ] No invented data, credentials, tests, or citations.
- [ ] Human readability and practical usefulness reviewed.
- [ ] SEO, AEO and GEO basics checked without keyword stuffing.
- [ ] Originality and source review recorded in the pull request.
- [ ] Technical build, links, structured data, mobile and accessibility checks passed.

## 6. Scope

This standard applies to all TEZZ CONNECT Knowledge Hub guides, Blog articles, landing-page educational content, translations, substantial content refreshes, and future editorial contributions. The Blog and Knowledge Hub remain separate content areas with their own audiences and purposes.
